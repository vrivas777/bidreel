# BidReel — VFX Bidding

A free, **offline, single-file** VFX bidding tool for the film & VFX community.
No installs, no accounts, no cloud. Open one HTML file and start building a bid.

**▶ Live app:** https://vrivas777.github.io/bidreel/

## Why

Bidding VFX work usually means a fragile spreadsheet that only one person understands.
BidReel is a self-contained estimator you can send to anyone: it runs entirely in the
browser, keeps every number local to your machine, and exports a clean client-ready
document when you're done.

## Features

- **Project setup** — studio, jurisdiction, currency, dates, discount and tax-rebate estimate.
- **Rates & roles** — per-department bid rates plus supervision and production roles.
- **Assets & shots** — line-item breakdown with complexity, labor and compute costs.
- **Totals** — full rollup with discount, rebate and payment milestones.
- **Charts** — visual breakdown of where the money goes.
- **Compare** — weigh bid scenarios side by side.
- **Export** — one-click **PDF** (client-ready) and **CSV**.
- **Save / Load** — your whole bid lives in a portable `.json` file.

## Privacy

Everything runs on your machine. There is no server, no tracking and no network
call — your bid data never leaves your computer. Move a bid between machines by
saving the `.json` and loading it on the other side.

## How to use

1. Open the [live app](https://vrivas777.github.io/bidreel/), **or** download
   `index.html` and double-click it — it works straight from your desktop.
2. Fill in **Project**, set your **Rates & roles**, then add **Assets** and **Shots**.
3. Review **Totals** and **Charts**.
4. **Export PDF** for the client, or **Save bid** to keep a `.json` you can reload later.

## Run it locally

No build step. Download the single file and open it:

```
index.html
```

That's the whole app.
