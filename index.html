Help & guide

Everything BidReel does, and how each part works. All data lives only in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Studio name & branding

The name at the top-left that reads YOUR STUDIO NAME is editable — click it and type your studio / company / boutique name. It appears on the PDF letterhead. You can also set it, plus an optional tagline and contact / website line, under Project → Studio / company name. Those two extra lines show under your studio name on the exported PDF.

Projects (multiple clients)

The Project bar under the top menu lets you keep many bids in one workspace — e.g. one for Marvel, one for Sony, one for Universal — and jump between them without closing the app.

  • Dropdown — switch the active project.
  • + New — start a blank bid.
  • Duplicate — copy the current bid as a starting point.
  • Delete — remove the current bid (there is always at least one).
  • Load (top bar) adds a saved .json into this list, so imported bids accumulate and can be switched and compared.

Project tab

Project name, client, dates and the financial/tax section. Work days available drives overhead. The Rebate jurisdiction dropdown auto-fills a current headline VFX incentive rate (still editable), and Rebate handling decides whether the rebate is subtracted from the client total (you claim it) or shown for information only (the client/production claims it). The Payment schedule is fully editable — any split you like, as long as it totals 100%.

Rates & roles tab

Departments are billed per day inside each asset/shot; set the average day rate, markup and type (Labor or Compute — Compute is kept out of the rebate labor base). Add or remove departments freely. Overhead per day × work days, and Setup is a one-time flat cost per new team member. Roles, supervision & production is where you bill people by time — VFX Producer, VFX Supervisor, Coordinator, Editorial, I/O, on-set crew — as rate × (1 + markup) × quantity (Day / Week / Month / Flat). These add to the grand total.

Assets & Shots tabs

  • Detailed vs Summary — Detailed shows the per-department day columns for data entry; Summary hides them for a clean read or a client screen-share.
  • Thumbnail — click the image box on a row to upload a picture or paste a direct image link, to identify shots fast.
  • Complexity — a dropdown (Low / Medium / High / Hero) with a Custom… option to type your own. It is a label only; it does not affect cost — cost comes from the day counts.

Totals & Charts

Totals summarizes the whole bid and breaks it down by department. Charts shows bid days by department, cost by department (with the highest and lowest called out) and a projected cash-flow line from your payment schedule.

Compare

Pick any two projects (A and B) from the workspace and see totals side by side with the difference, each in its own currency.

Import & export

  • Save bid / Load — save the current bid as a .json (the full, re-loadable format) and load it back on any computer. Load adds it to your project list.
  • Import CSV — bring in a list of assets/shots. Use a header row with Kind, Name, Type, Complexity and one column per department (matching your department names). Rows with Kind = Shot go to Shots; everything else to Assets. From Excel, use Save As → CSV to prepare a file.
  • Export CSV — a flat sheet of the current bid.
  • Export Excel — a structured .xlsx workbook with separate sheets: Summary, Assets, Shots, Roles, Departments, Payments (numbers as real numbers).
  • Export PDF — a client-ready bid document (use your browser's “Save as PDF” in the print dialog).

Saving & privacy

BidReel is a single offline file. Your workspace is auto-saved in this browser only. To move between computers, use Save bid and Load. Uploaded thumbnails are stored inside the .json, so many heavy images make the file larger — image links are lighter.
